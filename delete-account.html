At Split Your Trip, we respect your right to control your personal data. In accordance with Google Play's User Data and Account Deletion policies, this page provides clear, accessible instructions for requesting the permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal records.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your account and personal data using either of the two methods below:

1 Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion

You can permanently delete your account directly inside the app at any time:

  1. Open the Split Your Trip app on your device.
  2. Tap on your Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your choice in the confirmation dialog.

Your account, profile information, and authentication access are deleted immediately.

2 Option 2: Request Deletion via Email (No App Required)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can request account deletion by emailing our support team:

  • Send an email to: splityourtrip1@gmail.com
  • Subject line: Request Account Deletion - Split Your Trip
  • Include: The email address or phone number registered with your Split Your Trip account.

Our support team will verify your identity and process your deletion request within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once your account and data are permanently removed.

Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained

Data Category Action Taken Retention Details
User Profile
(Name, Email, Profile Picture, Bio)		 PERMANENTLY DELETED Deleted immediately (or within 30 days for email requests). No copies retained.
Authentication Records
(Password hashes, Google Sign-in tokens)		 PERMANENTLY DELETED Deleted immediately from our database and Supabase Auth.
Notification Tokens & Device Links PERMANENTLY DELETED FCM/Expo push tokens unlinked and removed from database.
Private Trips & Uploaded Receipts PERMANENTLY DELETED Trips where you are the sole member are deleted entirely including receipt images.
Historical Group Expense Line Items
(Shared Trips with other active users)		 ANONYMIZED To preserve the financial balance ledger for other participants who spent money, historical expense splits remain calculated but your name is replaced with "Former Member" and your personal info is wiped.

Retention Period

Once an account is deleted, your personal profile and authentication access are eliminated immediately. Any residual database backup snapshots are automatically purged according to our standard 30-day rolling backup cycle, after which no trace of your personal data remains.
