Privacy Policy

Application: Split Your Trip (com.splityourtrip)
Last Updated: October 14, 2026

Welcome to Split Your Trip ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a transparent, secure experience when using our mobile application (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used and shared, and your rights regarding your personal information in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

Core Promise: We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your personal information for third-party advertising or commercial brokerages.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Split Your Trip, we may collect the following types of information:

A. Account & Authentication Information

When you create an account using email/password or Google Sign-In, we collect your:

  • Name & Email Address: Used to create and secure your user account and verify your identity.
  • Profile Picture / Avatar: To personalize your experience and help co-travelers recognize you in shared trips.

B. Trips, Expenses & Financial Split Data

To provide our expense-splitting services, you may provide:

  • Trip titles, destinations, descriptions, and participant lists.
  • Expense details (amounts, categories, currencies, payer, and members involved in the split).
  • Settlement records, payment confirmation notes, and optional UPI ID details for settling balances.
  • Receipt or bill images that you choose to upload with an expense.

C. Device Permissions & Optional Data

The App may request access to certain features on your device only when needed:

  • Contacts (Optional - READ_CONTACTS): If you choose to grant access, we use your device contacts strictly to let you search and select friends to invite to a trip. We do not upload your address book to our servers, and we never share your contacts with third parties.
  • Camera & Media / Storage (Optional): Used only when you take a photo of an expense receipt or choose an image from your gallery as a trip cover or bill attachment.
  • Push Notifications (Optional): We collect your device push token to send timely notifications about newly added expenses, updated settlements, or trip chat messages.
  • Location (Optional): Used only if you choose to tag an expense or trip with a location name.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To calculate balances, splits, and settlements accurately among trip participants.
  • To synchronize real-time updates and notifications across all members of a shared trip.
  • To authenticate your identity and maintain the security of your account.
  • To provide customer support and troubleshoot technical issues.

3. How We Share Your Information

We only share information under strict and limited circumstances:

  • With Trip Participants: Any expenses, receipts, or comments you add to a trip are shared only with the other verified members of that specific trip.
  • Trusted Third-Party Service Providers: We work with trusted infrastructure providers that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements:
    • Supabase: For secure database management, user authentication, and encrypted data storage.
    • Google Identity Services: For secure OAuth sign-in authentication.
    • Expo & Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): For delivering push notifications to your mobile device.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit, token-based authentication, and restricted database row-level security policies (RLS). While no internet transmission is 100% secure, we continuously monitor and protect your data against unauthorized access.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal information as long as your account is active. You have full control over your data:

  • Editing/Deleting Trips & Expenses: You can edit or remove any expense or trip you created directly inside the App at any time.
  • Complete Account & Data Deletion: You can request full deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time by navigating to your profile settings or emailing us at splityourtrip.app@gmail.com. Upon receiving your verified request, all personal account data, profile details, and private records will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Split Your Trip is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete it.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
